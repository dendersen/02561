Part 6: Shading Questions

a) Shading:
Flat uses one face normal and color per triangle.
Gouraud lights vertices and interpolates colors.
Phong interpolates positions and normals, then lights each fragment.

Flat shading uses
normalize(cross(p2 - p1, p3 - p1)) for the whole face.

Phong gives better highlights because it evaluates them at every fragment.

b) Directional and point lights:
A directional light has one constant direction. A point light has a position, so its direction changes across the object.

c) Eye position:
It changes the view direction and specular highlight, but not ambient or diffuse light.

d) Specular term (0, 0, 0):
Specular highlights disappear.

e) Larger shininess exponent alpha:
The highlight becomes smaller and sharper.

f) Coordinate space:
Lighting uses object/world space. The MVP only projects positions for display.
