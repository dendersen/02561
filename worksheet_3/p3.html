Part 2: All three cubes share one pinhole camera with a 45-degree vertical field of view:
M_st * P_perspective * V

The model transforms are applied separately before the shared view transformation:
One-point: T_center * S * T_left
Two-point: T_center * S * R_y * T_right
Three-point: T_center * S * R_y * R_x * T_bottom

The rightmost model matrix acts first. The three transformed cubes are combined into one vertex buffer and rendered in one pass.
